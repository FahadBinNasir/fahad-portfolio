Independent product · 2026 · In progress

ApplyAI — AI Career Copilot

Problem

Tailoring every job application by hand is slow — and most "auto-apply" tools invent qualifications you don't have.

Approach

An assistant-native pipeline: it ingests job posts from WhatsApp, scores them against a verified career profile, drafts tailored CVs and cover letters using only real qualifications, screens for scam signals — and never submits without human approval.

Result

Live in production at applyai.fahadbinnasir.tech; Claude for Startups application submitted.

Independent product · 2026

AuthentiQ — Secure Interview Workspace

Problem

Remote interviews are easy to fake — and hard to audit afterward.

Approach

Next.js 15 + FastAPI with OTP email auth and role-based access, WebRTC live interview rooms, and integrity-event capture that compiles timestamped evidence reports with CSV export. Human-review-first throughout.

Result

Working end-to-end prototype: real-time rooms, evidence timelines, exportable reports.

  • Next.js 15
  • FastAPI
  • WebRTC
  • Cloudflare Workers

Final Year Project · 2026 · In progress

Unified Knowledge Brain — Governed RAG Platform

Problem

Team knowledge lives scattered across GitHub, Gmail, Drive, and Slack — unsearchable and ungoverned.

Approach

Multi-tenant platform (FastAPI + Next.js, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Redis) ingesting six sources via OAuth and webhooks, with PII redaction, five-tier RBAC, encrypted credential storage, and GDPR-compliant deletion.

Result

In active development as my Final Year Project I.
